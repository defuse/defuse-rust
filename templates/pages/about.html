About Defuse Security

Defuse Security is Taylor Hornby's personal homepage for security research, consulting services, and a whole bunch of other random stuff.

I build useful tools for security engineers like the Online x86 Assembler/Disassembler, and I've recently been exploring a niche of "high assurance AI coding" (if that isn't an oxymoron) by creating tools like vfy while simultaneously developing a claude-based tool for cryptographic security audits.

I have a degree in Computer Science and a Masters in Quantum Information. Most of my career has been centered around auditing cryptography-related software, my most recent project being to provide audits and community security support to the Zcash community. I'm currently a board member of the Zcash Foundation.

What I enjoy most of all is thinking critically about systems to understand their intricate behavior, then finding clever ways to exploit those intricacies to make the system do something unexpected. I also like cryptography for its ability to level power imbalances through decentralization and to increase our freedom by giving us privacy to express our deepest thoughts to only those who we trust. I'm also interested in complexity theory, mathematical logic, physics, and philosophy of mind.

If you would like to hire me for a security audit, see my Security Consulting page. I specialize in auditing all sorts of cryptography-related protocols and software, but I do general application security audits as well.
